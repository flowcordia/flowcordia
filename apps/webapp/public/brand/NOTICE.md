# Flowcordia Identity Assets

Black and white Cordia flower logos copied without modification from
https://github.com/ahamdjin/flowcordia-site at commit
345af0e5e413aa44ad4f5e156e026c64347570c6.

The site uses these same SVGs as its theme-aware favicons. Its MIT license is
preserved in LICENSE.flowcordia-site.txt. The app adapts the site's restrained
neutral surfaces, flower mark, and black/white actions while retaining semantic
colors for workflow navigation and execution states.
